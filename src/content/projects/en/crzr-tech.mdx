## Context

**cRZR** is my tech services brand in Cancún, Quintana Roo. It covers three areas: device **repair**, **web development** and **consulting**. The site is its storefront, and it had to show at a glance that there's someone behind it who understands both hardware and software: *"Optimizing hardware. Deploying software."*

## The Challenge

- **Two worlds, one message:** device repair and software development are usually sold separately. The brand had to present them as a single, coherent offer.
- **A visual identity with character:** move away from the typical "tech support" site and build a technical, minimalist look without hurting readability.
- **Lightweight interactivity:** smooth animations and interactive details on mobile and desktop.
- **Turn visits into conversations:** a short path from "I'm interested" to talking to me directly.

## The Solution

- **Typographic hero:** the cRZR logotype in geometric and pixel type, with the tagline typed out live behind a terminal cursor.
- **Floating Tetris-like pieces** around the hero with scroll-linked parallax, as a metaphor for putting hardware and software together.
- **Custom cursor, noise texture and smooth scroll** to reinforce the feeling of sitting at a *workstation*.
- **Two clear calls to action** above the fold: *Contact now* and *See services*.
- **Direct contact over WhatsApp**, for clients who'd rather talk than fill out long forms.
- **Next.js + React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4**, with metadata, sitemap and Open Graph generated by the framework for local SEO.

## Outcome

- The brand has its own production website that I designed and built end to end.
- Visitors can message me on WhatsApp in one click, right from the first screen.
