## Context

**cRZR** is my tech services brand in Cancún, Q. Roo. It covers three fronts: device **repair**, **web development** and **consulting**. The site is its commercial face, and it had to convey within seconds that there's someone behind it who understands both hardware and software: *"Optimizing hardware. Deploying software."*

## The Challenge

- **Two worlds, one message:** device repair and software development are usually sold separately. The brand had to present them as one coherent offer.
- **A visual identity with character:** move away from the typical "tech support" site and build its own technical, minimalist aesthetic without losing legibility.
- **Interactivity that stays light:** fluid animations and interactive details on mobile and desktop.
- **Turn visits into conversations:** a short path from "I'm interested" to talking to me directly.

## The Solution

- **Typographic hero:** the cRZR logo in a geometric, pixelated typeface, with the tagline typed live behind a terminal cursor.
- **Floating Tetris-like pieces** around the hero, with scroll-linked parallax, as a metaphor for assembling hardware and software.
- **Custom cursor, noise texture and smooth scroll** to reinforce the feeling of sitting at a *workstation*.
- **Two clear calls to action** from the first screen: *Contact now* and *See services*.
- **Direct WhatsApp contact**, for clients who'd rather talk than fill out long forms.
- **Next.js + React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4**, with metadata, sitemap and Open Graph generated by the framework for local search.

## Result

- A site that works as the brand's business card: the website itself shows the level of design and development it offers.
- A recognizable, consistent visual identity across the site, social media and contact channels.
- A one-step contact funnel, from first glance to conversation.
