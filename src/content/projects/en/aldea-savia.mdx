## Context

Onix Living launched **Aldea Savia**, a premium residential development with a sustainable focus in Tulum, Mexico. The sales team used to manage and present lot and unit availability through manual spreadsheets and long PDF documents. That process needed constant updates and often left outdated information in front of the client.

The goal was to turn that experience into a real-time interactive platform: the main sales tool on site, conveying the project's values through a "Quiet Luxury" editorial aesthetic while streamlining inventory management.

## The Challenge

- **Simple inventory management:** the admin team needed to update availability (available, reserved, sold) instantly and easily, without learning complex management platforms.
- **Flawless performance in the field:** sales advisors use the tool at the construction site in Tulum, where mobile connectivity is often unstable.
- **Immersive, fluid interactivity:** advanced touch navigation (3D models and interactive plans with zoom and pan) that runs smoothly on mobile devices.
- **Cohesive brand design:** maps and location tools had to match the brand's monochrome, earthy palette instead of generic map styles.

## The Solution

- **Real-time sync:** the platform connects directly to a Google Sheets spreadsheet, so any change made by the admin team shows up on the site instantly.
- **Immersive exploration:**
  - A custom interactive engine lets users explore the development through guided tours and dynamic plans, on touch screens and desktop alike.
  - The interface highlights the status of each unit visually and intuitively, making the buyer's decision easier.
- **Premium design and technical performance:** built from scratch, avoiding generic templates and heavy tools, for a refined interface with smooth transitions in line with the "Quiet Luxury" concept.
- **Custom maps:** map views fully adapted to the project's identity, seamlessly integrated with the rest of the interface.

## Results and Value

- **Speed and accessibility:** the platform is designed to load fast on mobile networks, so the sales team never loses the moment in front of the client.
- **Accurate sales:** the direct connection to Google Sheets removed operational friction and cross-availability errors. The admin team updates a simple document and the platform does the rest.
- **Premium user experience:** a fluid, intuitive and visually striking interface that elevates how Aldea Savia is perceived and eases the sales process.
- **Measurable web quality:** 100 in Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO on mobile Lighthouse (measured in September 2026).
