## Context

**Andiani** manages more than 250 vacation rentals across Mexico. Behind every stay there's cleaning, maintenance, guest messages, owner payouts and a team spread across several cities. The dashboard ("tablero") is the internal tool where all of that operation lives in one place, in real time.

We built it as a team of two: the owner, who defines how the business runs, and me, in charge of the technical side. Since June 2026 I've made more than 2,000 commits to the project.

*Since it's an internal tool, the images in this case study are illustrative and show no real data.*

## The Challenge

- **Everything in one place:** operations, guests, finance and team had to live in the same platform, including an in-house message inbox to replace the third-party tool in use.
- **Per-person permissions:** each team member sees and edits only what they're responsible for (finance, HR, operations), and that rule is enforced on the server, not just in the UI.
- **Real time, in the field:** cleaning and maintenance work from their phones; a change has to show up instantly on every screen.
- **Guest support around the clock:** Guesty, WhatsApp and Meta conversations in a single inbox, with an agent that can reply.

## The Solution

- **Modules per area:** property operations, cleaning and maintenance, guest inbox, finance and treasury, owners, human resources, KPIs and training.
- **Andi, an AI agent:** answers guests from the inbox and runs on its own backend, separate from the rest.
- **Installable field portal (PWA):** the cleaning and maintenance crew works from their phones, no app store needed.
- **Team gamification:** points, a weekly ranking and per-person KPIs to recognize good work.
- **Owner NPS survey:** a public link to rate the service, with results in the dashboard.
- **Architecture:** React 18 + strict TypeScript + Tailwind on Vite, real-time Firestore, 100+ Cloud Functions and Google sign-in restricted to the company domain, with permissions enforced by Firestore rules.

## Engineering

- **Three levels of tests:** pure logic with `node --test`, store and components with Vitest, and Firestore rules against the emulator.
- **CI ratchets that only go down:** max lines per file, max bundle weight per screen, minimum coverage and ESLint warnings. If something grows, the code gets fixed; the number doesn't go up.
- **Demo mode:** seeded in-memory data to build and test screens without touching production.

## Result

- The dashboard is in production and is the Andiani team's day-to-day tool.
- The in-house inbox replaced the third-party messaging tool and is integrated with the rest of the operation.
- More than 2,000 commits of my own since June 2026, with CI guarding quality on every change.
