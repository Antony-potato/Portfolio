## Context

**Pocky** is a modern, Tamagotchi-style virtual pet: it runs in any browser, installs as an app and keeps its state synced across devices in real time. The idea: experiment with PWA + Firebase and see how far the browser can be pushed as a mobile app runtime.

## Challenge

- Real-time state between iPhone and Android with no backend of my own.
- Installable as an app from Safari (PWAs on iOS have their quirks).
- Service Workers for background tasks (the little guy gets sad if you ignore him).
- Human latency: animations that feel alive, not robotic.

## Solution

- **Firebase Realtime DB** for sync with presence.
- Manifest + optimized icons for Add to Home Screen on iOS and Android.
- Service Worker with a `stale-while-revalidate` cache strategy.
- Animation loop with `requestAnimationFrame` and interpolation.

## Result

- Works as an installable app on iPhone and Android without an app store.
- State synced across devices in under 200 ms.
- Zero custom backend: all the logic lives in the client + Firebase.
